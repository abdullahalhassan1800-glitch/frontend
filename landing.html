Features

Everything You Need to Manage Orders

From order tracking to customer management, OMS has all the tools to scale your business efficiently.

Order Tracking

Track every order from placement to delivery. Real-time status updates, shipping integration with Shiprocket, and automated notifications.

Customer Management

Complete CRM with customer profiles, order history, segments, and company management. Know your customers better.

Product Inventory

Manage products, track inventory levels, handle purchase orders, and manage stock transfers between locations.

Analytics Dashboard

Real-time sales trends, revenue reports, and performance metrics. Make data-driven decisions with beautiful charts.

Agent Monitoring

Monitor agent activity, track performance, and manage team workflows. Perfect for call centers and support teams.

Push Notifications

Instant push notifications for new orders, status changes, and important updates. Never miss a critical event.
